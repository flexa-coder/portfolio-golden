FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Let’s clear up
a few things.

If you’re not sure where to start, that’s okay. Here are a few common questions from businesses exploring digital support.

What kind of businesses do you work with?+

We’re focused on shopkeepers, small businesses, local service providers, and entrepreneurs who want to improve how they present their business online.

Do I need to know which service I need?+

No. Tell us what you’re trying to improve—like presenting your services, keeping social profiles active, or getting more enquiries—and we can discuss suitable options.

How much do your services cost?+

Pricing depends on the work and its scope. We’ll discuss the requirements, deliverables, and budget with you before work starts.

Can you set up and manage my social media profiles?+

We can discuss profile setup, content planning, creative support, and ongoing management. Your business should retain ownership of its accounts and credentials.

Can you run ads for my business?+

We can discuss campaign planning and ad creative. Advertising spend is separate from our service fee, and results depend on many factors, so specific sales outcomes can’t be guaranteed.

How do we get started?+

Complete the enquiry form and choose how to send the message. We’ll need your contact details and a little information about your business goals.
